## Shared style guidance (paste into every prompt)

> Cinematic documentary photography, photorealistic, Niger Delta setting (West Africa). Warm muted color palette with deep shadows and golden highlights. Natural lighting, slight film grain. 4:3 aspect ratio. Respectful, dignified portrayal — never caricatured. People depicted are Nigerian / Black African.
